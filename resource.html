🖼️ Coding & Study

💡 Top Study Tips for Beginners

  • Type every example manually — don't just copy-paste code.
  • Save and refresh your browser after every change to test results.
  • Build small parts first, then combine them into bigger projects.
  • Keep all your project files organized in separate folders.
  • Don't fear errors — fixing mistakes is the best way to learn!
